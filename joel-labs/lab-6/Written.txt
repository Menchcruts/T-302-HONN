One of the known problems with N-Tier architecture shines through very strongly in this project, what is that problem? Explain.

The N-Tier sinkhole anti-pattern is when all layers are thin wrappers for the database and the service layer provides no real extra logic.
For example in movie_service.py, all MovieService does is wrap the methods get_all and create_movie for the MovieRepository without doing anything else.
This can also be seen in all the other services.
